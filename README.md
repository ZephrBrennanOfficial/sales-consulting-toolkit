## 📋 Overview

The **Sales Consulting Toolkit** is a practical framework designed to explore, standardize, and accelerate revenue operations for B2B sales teams. Built on modern, buyer-centric methodology (MEDDPICC, Sandler, Value-Based Selling), this toolkit bridges the gap between sales strategy concepts and front-line execution.

Whether studying sales funnel diagnostics, practicing discovery protocols, or building ROI calculators, this repository provides copy-paste ready assets and calculation models built for learning by doing.

---

## 🎯 Who This Is For

- **Sales Learners & Aspiring Reps:** Practical templates for learning discovery execution, pipeline mapping, and value modeling.
- **Founders & Revenue Leaders:** Early-stage executives looking to explore structured B2B sales playbooks.
- **Account Executives & SDRs:** Front-line reps looking for outreach templates and objection-handling frameworks.
- **Sales Operations Enthusiasts:** Process framework maps covering stage gates, entrance/exit criteria, and tech stack integrations.

## 🚀 How to Use This Toolkit

1. **Conduct an Initial Audit:** Use [`01-sales-audit-framework.md`](./01-sales-audit-framework.md) to score a sales engine across 7 critical stages and identify primary bottlenecks.
2. **Align the Sales Process:** Benchmark pipeline flows against [`05-sales-process-roadmap.md`](./05-sales-process-roadmap.md) to ensure clean stage gates and measurable exit criteria.
3. **Upgrade Call Execution:** Implement [`02-discovery-call-template.md`](./02-discovery-call-template.md) for pre-call preparation and systematic pain discovery.
4. **Arm Reps with Tactical Tools:** Deploy [`04-outreach-templates.md`](./04-outreach-templates.md) for outbound sequences and keep [`06-objection-handling-quick-reference.md`](./06-objection-handling-quick-reference.md) open during roleplays or calls to address hesitations smoothly.
5. **Quantify Value:** Open [`03-roi-calculator.html`](./03-roi-calculator.html) in any browser during practice presentations to demonstrate the financial impact of conversion rate optimization.
