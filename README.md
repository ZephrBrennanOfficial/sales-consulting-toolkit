## 📋 Overview

The **Sales Consulting Toolkit** is an end-to-end framework designed to diagnose, standardize, and accelerate revenue operations for B2B sales teams and growing enterprises. Built on modern, buyer-centric methodology (MEDDPICC, Sandler, Value-Based Selling), this toolkit bridge the gap between high-level sales strategy and tactical front-line execution.

Whether auditing an underperforming sales funnel, training entry-to-mid-level account executives, or establishing repeatable discovery protocols, this repository provides immediate, copy-paste ready assets and calculation models.

---

## 🎯 Who This Is For

- **Sales Consultants & Advisors:** Ready-to-use client artifacts for initial diagnostic audits, discovery execution, and ROI calculations.
- **Founders & Revenue Leaders:** Early-stage executives looking to build a structured, scalable B2B sales playbook from ground zero.
- **Account Executives & SDRs:** Front-line reps seeking high-conversion outreach templates and a reliable objection-handling framework.
- **Sales Operations Professionals:** Process architects mapping stage gates, entrance/exit criteria, and tech stack integrations.

## 🚀 How to Use This Toolkit

1. **Conduct an Initial Audit:** Use [`01-sales-audit-framework.md`](./01-sales-audit-framework.md) to score your or your client's sales engine across the 7 critical stages. Identify the primary bottleneck (e.g., Lead Gen vs. Discovery vs. Closing).
2. **Align the Sales Process:** Benchmark your funnel against [`05-sales-process-roadmap.md`](./05-sales-process-roadmap.md) to ensure clean stage gates and measurable exit criteria.
3. **Upgrade Call Execution:** Implement [`02-discovery-call-template.md`](./02-discovery-call-template.md) for pre-call preparation and systematic pain discovery.
4. **Arm Reps with Tactical Tools:** Deploy [`04-outreach-templates.md`](./04-outreach-templates.md) for outbound sequences and keep [`06-objection-handling-quick-reference.md`](./06-objection-handling-quick-reference.md) open during calls to address hesitations smoothly.
5. **Quantify Value:** Open [`03-roi-calculator.html`](./03-roi-calculator.html) in any browser during executive presentations to demonstrate the direct financial impact of conversion rate optimization.
